# The demo video

Start here, then work from the other two files.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`voiceover.md`](voiceover.md) | **The words.** Thirteen blocks, one per screen, in three lengths. Paste each into ElevenLabs and download it. |
| [`SHOTLIST.md`](SHOTLIST.md) | **The edit.** Where each clip goes and how long that screen has to stay up. |
| [`mix.sh`](mix.sh) | Optional. Lays all thirteen clips over your recording in one command. |
| `clips/` | Where your `01.mp3` … `13.mp3` go. |
| `archive/` | The first attempt, kept for reference. It was too short. |

---

## The order

1. **Time your recording.** You need one number: how long it runs.
2. Open `voiceover.md`, pick **Short**, **Core** or **Long** from the table at
   the top, and generate the thirteen clips at the settings it lists.
3. Open `SHOTLIST.md` and place each clip.
4. Export at 1080p, 30fps.
5. Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted**, not Private. A judge has to be able to
   open it without an account.
6. Send me the URL and I will put it on the site and in the README.

---

## The one rule

The picture leads. Each clip starts when its screen appears; it is fine for it
to finish over the next one. If a clip is still going two screens later, slow
the footage down. Do not cut the voice, and never speed the picture up to fit.

A viewer needs about four seconds to read a screen they have never seen. It will
feel slow to you because you already know what is on it.

---

## Why there is no music

The script is full of numbers, and every one of them is checkable. Music makes
them harder to hear and makes a factual walkthrough sound like an advert, which
is the opposite of the argument the product is making.
