# Shot list

The recorded tape is **75 seconds**. The voiceover is **165 words**, which reads
in about **76 seconds** at a normal pace. They fit.

**Before you record anything**, open https://mandate-coral.vercel.app and check
that the two numbers in the first lines of `voiceover.txt` still match the
homepage. The site re-counts itself; the script is a caption. If they differ,
change the words in the ElevenLabs box to whatever the site says.

## If you are recording by hand

Same order. The only rule that matters: **wait longer than feels natural on
every page.** Three full seconds after a page loads before you scroll. You will
feel like you are being slow. You are not.

1. `https://mandate-coral.vercel.app` — wait 4 seconds. Scroll down slowly to
   the four tiles. Stop.
2. Click **Check a position** in the top bar. Wait 2 seconds.
3. Type `7331221` into the box. Type it, do not paste. Click **Check it**.
4. Wait 4 seconds on the result. Scroll down slowly to the agent cards. Stop.
5. Click **Agents** in the top bar, then the **Answered when we called it**
   filter. Wait 3 seconds.
6. Click any agent that shows a green dot. Wait 4 seconds. Scroll down through
   the six checks slowly.
7. Click **Activity** in the top bar. Wait 3 seconds. Scroll a little.
8. Click any **Receipt** button. Wait 4 seconds. Scroll down through the marks.
9. Click **How we check** in the top bar. Wait 3 seconds. Stop recording.

## Two things that will ruin the take

**Your cursor wobbling.** If you are recording by hand, move the mouse to where
you are going, then stop moving it, then read. A cursor drifting around while
someone talks is distracting.

**A notification.** Turn them off before you start. A message popping up on
screen in a submission video is the one thing a judge will remember.
